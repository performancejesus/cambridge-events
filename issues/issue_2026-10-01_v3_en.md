# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

This fortnight's big story is Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, marked around Cambridge with a tribute concert, an exhibition, a biographer's talk and even a football match with a personal connection to the family. Elsewhere the Corn Exchange is doing heavy lifting — Don Broco, Simon Amstell, Alison Moyet and Roachford & Hue and Cry all pass through — and the city gets its first UNIQLO and its first cat café.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge's most famous psychedelic son would have turned 80 this month, and the city marks it with a concert, a film, a talk and an art show — plus, oddly, a football match.

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · price not listed  
A home league match with a personal edge: proceeds from the day go to mental health charities, after Syd Barrett's sister spoke about the cause tied to his birthday. Kick-off is at 3pm. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, at the venue where he gave his final live performance in 1972. The line-up draws on the Clowns and Jugglers tribute album, with a light show from The Mad Alchemist. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Meet the Director: Roddy Bogawa + Have You Got It Yet? Screening** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace and Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); screening price not listed  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about his documentary at 5pm, followed by a screening at the Picturehouse at 8.30pm the same day. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett – A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
A talk by Barrett's biographer, author of the book of the same name. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · price not listed  
Paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists tracing Barrett's visual art alongside his music. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

## Weekdays: concerts, theatre, comedy

**Don Broco** — Tue 6 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £42.50–£48.00  
The Bedford rock quartet, known for blending post-hardcore with pop hooks, play the Corn Exchange as part of a run of regional theatre shows. Expect a heavier, more physical show than their festival slots. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/don-broco)

**Simon Amstell: I Love It Here** — Thu 8 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £23.00–£30.00  
Comedian and filmmaker Simon Amstell brings his new stand-up show to Cambridge after a sold-out London residency. Known for his self-deprecating, confessional style, expect material that's as much about therapy as about comedy. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/simon-amstell-i-love-it-here)

**Joe Jackson: Hope and Fury Tour** — Thu 1 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £35.50–£52.50  
The English singer-songwriter tours songs from across his career alongside his new album Hope and Fury. Jackson made his name with sharp new wave pop in the late 1970s before moving between jazz, classical and pop. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/joe-jackson-hope-and-fury-tour-2026)

**Alison Moyet** — Fri 9 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £38.50–£71.50  
Moyet's 2026 tour is built entirely around her Yazoo-era catalogue plus tracks from her more recent electronica albums. It's a rare chance to hear the Yazoo material live, since much of it is seldom performed outside her old duo's reunion tours. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/alison-moyet-2)

**Steve Hackett: Best of Genesis & Solo Gems** — Fri 2 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £52.50–£68.00
