# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

Two weeks that mix a hometown tribute to Syd Barrett with big names at the Corn Exchange, Cambridge Footlights' autumn tour show, a clutch of new film releases and the usual harvest-season clutter of pumpkin patches and open gardens out in the villages.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80: Cambridge remembers

Syd Barrett, Pink Floyd's founding singer and guitarist, was born and later died in Cambridge; this year marks 80 years since his birth, and the city marks it with a concert, an exhibition, talks and a film screening.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking the year Syd Barrett, Pink Floyd's founding songwriter and guitarist, would have turned 80. Kula Shaker headline with Soft Machine also on the bill, alongside a light show from The Mad Alchemist, and it's held at the Corn Exchange, where Barrett gave his final live performance in 1972. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · prices on the website  
A small exhibition of paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists exploring Barrett's visual art and its afterlife, running for the week of the anniversary celebrations. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett — A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's acclaimed biographer Rob Chapman talks about his life and career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Roddy Bogawa: talk and outtakes, then the film** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cambridge / Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); prices on the website (screening)  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his documentary Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd and shows unseen outtakes at 17:00, then the finished film screens at the Arts Picturehouse at 20:30 the same evening. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
An ordinary League One home fixture with an unexpected link: according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, proceeds from this match go to mental health charities — 10 October is also World Mental Health Day. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
