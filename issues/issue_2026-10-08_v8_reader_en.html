What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct

Cambridge turns 80 candles into a citywide tribute this fortnight, as Syd Barrett's birthday brings a Corn Exchange concert, gallery talks and even a football match for mental health charities. Beyond that, look out for Roachford and Hue & Cry, Nish Kumar, Antony Szmierek and a run of big Corn Exchange names on both weekends.

Theme of the week: Cambridge remembers Syd Barrett at 80

Pink Floyd's founder was born and grew up in Cambridge, and this week the city marks what would have been his 80th birthday with a tribute concert at the very hall where he played his last gig, plus gallery talks, a documentary screening — and, via an unexpected route, a football match for mental health charities.

A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations

Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132

Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a concert at the Corn Exchange, the very hall where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last live performance, in February 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine headline a bill of artists reinterpreting his songs, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities.
