# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

Cambridge turns 80 candles into a citywide tribute this fortnight, as Syd Barrett's birthday brings a Corn Exchange concert, gallery talks and even a football match for mental health charities. Beyond that, look out for Roachford and Hue & Cry, Nish Kumar, Antony Szmierek and a run of big Corn Exchange names on both weekends.

## Theme of the week: Cambridge remembers Syd Barrett at 80

Pink Floyd's founder was born and grew up in Cambridge, and this week the city marks what would have been his 80th birthday with a tribute concert at the very hall where he played his last gig, plus gallery talks, a documentary screening — and, via an unexpected route, a football match for mental health charities.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a concert at the Corn Exchange, the very hall where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last live performance, in February 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine headline a bill of artists reinterpreting his songs, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · prices on the website  
A small gallery show of original paintings and sketches by Barrett alongside work by contemporary artists responding to his art and music. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett – A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's acclaimed biographer Rob Chapman talks about his subject's career, part of the Openspace anniversary programme. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Meet the director + film screening: Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace (talk, 5pm) and Arts Picturehouse (screening, 8.30pm), Cambridge · £10 (talk); cinema price on booking  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his documentary on Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd, with unseen outtakes, at Openspace at 5pm; the film itself screens at the Arts Picturehouse that evening. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · price on booking  
An unexpected link to the week's theme: according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, this league fixture's proceeds go to mental health charities — 10 October also being World Mental Health Day. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
