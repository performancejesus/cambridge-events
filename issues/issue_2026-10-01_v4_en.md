# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

Cambridge's autumn gig calendar hits full throttle this fortnight — Don Broco, Simon Amstell, Alison Moyet and Steve Hackett all pack into the Corn Exchange within a week of each other, and the same room turns into a tribute to Syd Barrett on what would have been his 80th birthday. Add the Footlights' home run, a Roachford/Hue and Cry double bill, and half-term camps to book ahead, and there's plenty to plan around.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a concert at the Corn Exchange — the room where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last-ever gig in 1972 — plus an exhibition and a director's talk with film screening, all in the same week.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, staged at the very venue where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last-ever live performance in 1972. The bill includes Kula Shaker and other artists performing songs from the Clowns and Jugglers tribute album, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental-health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · price not listed  
A week-long exhibition of original paintings, sketches and contemporary artworks exploring Barrett's art and legacy, timed to run over his birthday. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett – A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's biographer Rob Chapman talks about his book on the musician. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Meet the Director + Screening: Have You Got It Yet?** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cambridge & Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); screening price not listed  
Director Roddy Bogawa discusses his documentary about Barrett and Pink Floyd at Openspace at 5pm, followed the same evening by a screening of the film itself at the Arts Picturehouse. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)
