# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 28 Sep – 11 Oct · the weekend: 3–4 Oct*

October opens with a packed Corn Exchange, Cambridge United playing twice at the Abbey Stadium, and two new free shows at the Fitzwilliam to wander round on a rainy afternoon. Out in the villages there's folk music, dowsing at an Iron Age hillfort and a 40th-birthday concert from the Sampson Orchestra — plenty of reasons to get out of the centre this fortnight.
