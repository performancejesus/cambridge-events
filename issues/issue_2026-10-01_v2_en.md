# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 28 Sep – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

The next two weeks bring one of the busiest stretches of gigs the Corn Exchange has seen in a while — Don Broco, Alison Moyet, Joe Jackson and more — alongside a whole weekend remembering Syd Barrett, Cambridge-born and Pink Floyd's founding songwriter, on what would have been his 80th birthday. Elsewhere, Roachford and Hue and Cry share a bill, Cambridge United host Blackpool on Syd Barrett day, and Cambridge's first UNIQLO has opened its doors.

## Theme of the week: Cambridge remembers Syd Barrett at 80

Syd Barrett, founding songwriter of Pink Floyd, was born in Cambridge and gave his last live performance at the Corn Exchange. The venue and several smaller Cambridge venues mark what would have been his 80th birthday with a concert, an exhibition, talks and film screenings across the weekend of 9–10 October.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
Cambridge Corn Exchange marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a concert on 10 October, in the same hall where he gave his final live performance back in 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine are among the acts, alongside artists from the new tribute album Clowns and Jugglers, and the proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · price not listed  
A gallery show tracing Barrett's visual art and its wider cultural impact, running through the anniversary weekend and beyond. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd** — Fri 9 Oct, 20:30 · Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · price not listed  
The 2023 documentary about Barrett and Pink Floyd gets a screening at the Arts Picturehouse. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)
