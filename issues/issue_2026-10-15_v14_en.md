# What's on in Cambridge — 15 Oct 2026

*Events 15 Oct – 25 Oct · weekends: 17–18 Oct and 24–25 Oct*

Two weeks of big names at the Corn Exchange (Nish Kumar, Michael Palin, Maisie Adam all play within the same fortnight), new shows opening at the Fitzwilliam, Halloween half-term activities at Audley End House and Gardens and Wicken Fen, and Cambridge United at home.

## Theme of the week: New seasons open at the Fitzwilliam

Over this fortnight the Fitzwilliam opens two new shows — An Enamel Revolution on 20 October and A Call to Art on 23 October — alongside its big Frank Bowling retrospective and the Mughal Songbook exhibition already running.

**Frank Bowling: Seeking the Sublime** — until 17 Jan 2027 · Fitzwilliam Museum, Cambridge · free  
Nearly seven decades of the British painter's work, from his figurative paintings of the 1960s to the large abstract canvases he makes today, tracing the influence of Titian, Turner and Tracey Emin on his painting. [More →](https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/plan-your-visit/exhibitions/frank-bowling-seeking-the-sublime)
*Why go: Free entry to a show covering nearly 70 years of one artist's career.*  

**An Enamel Revolution: China and the World** — 20 Oct – 21 Feb 2027 · Fitzwilliam Museum, Cambridge · free  
How Chinese workshops from the 1600s revolutionised enamel decoration on porcelain, glass and copper, including pieces adapted for export to European buyers. [More →](https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/plan-your-visit/exhibitions/an-enamel-revolution-china-and-the-world)
*Why go: Opens 20 October, free.*  

**A Call to Art: William Morris and the Pre-Raphaelites** — 23 Oct – 3 May 2027 · Fitzwilliam Museum, Cambridge · from £16, free for members  
Morris and the Pre-Raphaelites' designs, textiles and paintings, drawn from the museum's own collection, built around their belief that art could reshape society. [More →](https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/plan-your-visit/exhibitions/a-call-to-art-william-morris-and-the-pre-raphaelites)
*Why go: Opens 23 October.*  

**A Mughal Songbook: Art, Music and Empire** — until 7 Mar 2027 · Fitzwilliam Museum, Cambridge · free  
The rare Plowden manuscript of 77 Persian and Urdu songs from Mughal South Asia, around 1750–1850, shown alongside illustrations and sound. [More →](https://fitzmuseum.cam.ac.uk/plan-your-visit/exhibitions/a-mughal-songbook-art-music-and-empire)
*Why go: Free, runs to March 2027.*
