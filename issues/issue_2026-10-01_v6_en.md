# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a week of gigs, talks and a charity football match, while the weekend brings a big co-headline concert at the Corn Exchange, a 10K through the city centre and the Cambridge Poetry Festival.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

What would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday is marked across Cambridge this week with a concert at the Corn Exchange where the Pink Floyd founder played his last show, an exhibition, two talks and — coincidentally, on World Mental Health Day — a charity football match.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, staged at the Corn Exchange where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last live performance in February 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine are among the acts performing his songs, with a light show from The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · prices on the website  
Paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists exploring Syd Barrett's art and his influence on music and visual culture. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett — A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Acclaimed Syd Barrett biographer Rob Chapman talks about the musician's life and career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Roddy Bogawa: talk and outtakes, then Have You Got It Yet? screening** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cherry Hinton Road, then Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £11.00  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his Syd Barrett documentary and shows unseen outtakes, followed later the same day by a screening of the film itself, Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd, at the Arts Picturehouse. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
League Two football at the Abbey Stadium. According to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, this match falls on World Mental Health Day and all proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
