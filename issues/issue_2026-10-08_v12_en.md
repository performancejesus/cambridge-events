# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

Two weeks dominated by one anniversary: Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th year with a tribute concert, talks and an exhibition, while the Corn Exchange keeps the big names coming with Alison Moyet, Nish Kumar and The Beat.

## Theme of the week: Cambridge toasts Syd Barrett

This week the city marks the year of what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday — the Pink Floyd founder was born here and gave his last live performance here — with a tribute concert, talks, an exhibition and one unexpected football connection.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking the year of what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, in the city where the Pink Floyd founder was born and where he gave his last live performance. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine are among the acts performing his songs, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, Cambridge · prices on the website  
Paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists tracing Barrett's visual art and its influence, running through the anniversary week. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett — A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's biographer Rob Chapman, author of A Very Irregular Head, talks about his career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Have You Got It Yet?: meet the director + film screening** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace / Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); cinema price on booking  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his Barrett documentary and shows unseen outtakes (5pm, Openspace), followed the same evening by a screening of the finished film, Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd (8.30pm, Arts Picturehouse). [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
An ordinary League One fixture with an unexpected link to the week's theme: according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, Cambridge United are playing Blackpool on this day with proceeds going to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
