# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

This week Cambridge remembers its own psychedelic son — Syd Barrett would have turned 80 this year, and the Corn Exchange marks it with a tribute concert in the very hall where he played his last gig. Elsewhere, Nish Kumar on Saturday and The Beat on Sunday of the second weekend, Alice Cooper talks through his new memoir, and UNIQLO's first Cambridge store has just opened its doors.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge's most famous psychedelic son, Syd Barrett, founder of Pink Floyd, would have turned 80 this year — he was born in the city on 6 January 1946 and died here in 2006. This week the Corn Exchange, where he gave his very last concert in 1972, hosts a tribute, alongside a small gallery show, a director's talk with a screening, and even an unexpected football connection.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett – Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
The night's centrepiece: a concert of Barrett's songs in the hall where he played his very last gig, in 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine are among those performing his music, with a light show from The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd: director's talk and screening** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace / Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); prices on the website (screening)  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his 2023 documentary on Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd and shows unseen outtakes at 5pm at Openspace; the film itself screens at the Arts Picturehouse at 8.30pm the same evening. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, Cambridge · prices on the website  
A small show of original paintings, sketches and new work by contemporary artists inspired by Barrett's art and music. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
An otherwise ordinary League One fixture gains a Barrett connection: his sister Rosemary Breen has said Cambridge United are donating proceeds from this match to mental health charities — a nod to World Mental Health Day, which falls the same weekend. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
