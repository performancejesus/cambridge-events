What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct

Cambridge spends this fortnight marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a tribute concert, an exhibition and even a Cambridge United match for charity, while Roachford and Hue and Cry, Alison Moyet and Don Broco keep the Corn Exchange busy and a new Uniqlo and a cat café open their doors in town.

Theme of the week: Remembering Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday this week with a tribute concert at the venue of his final gig, a small exhibition, a biographer's talk and a documentary screening — and, almost by accident, a football match too.

A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations

Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132

A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, at the very venue where he gave his last live performance in 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine headline a bill of artists reinterpreting his songs, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist.
