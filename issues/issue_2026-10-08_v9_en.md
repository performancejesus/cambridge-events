# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

Two weeks bookended by big names at the Corn Exchange – Simon Amstell, Alice Cooper, Nish Kumar – and, right in the middle, Cambridge remembers its most famous lost son: Syd Barrett would have turned 80 this month, and the city marks it with a tribute concert, a small exhibition and even a football match played in his sister's name.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Syd Barrett, the founding songwriter of Pink Floyd, grew up in Cambridge and would have turned 80 this month; the city marks the occasion with a tribute concert, a small exhibition, talks and one unexpected football match.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, with Kula Shaker and Soft Machine among the artists performing his songs, plus a light show by The Mad Alchemist. It's held at the Corn Exchange, where Barrett gave his last live performance in February 1972; proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · prices on the website  
Original paintings and sketches, alongside work by contemporary artists responding to Barrett's art and legacy. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk: Rob Chapman on Syd Barrett** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Rob Chapman, author of the Barrett biography 'A Very Irregular Head', talks about his career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Meet the director + screening: Have You Got It Yet?** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cambridge / Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £10 (talk); cinema price on the website  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his documentary on Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd and shows unseen outtakes at 5pm; the film itself screens at the Arts Picturehouse at 8.30pm the same evening. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
An ordinary League Two fixture that turns out to have a connection: according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, Cambridge United are donating proceeds from this match to mental health charities — it also happens to fall on World Mental Health Day. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
