# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

This fortnight belongs to Syd Barrett: Cambridge marks what would have been his 80th birthday with a tribute concert, an exhibition and even a football match with a personal connection. Elsewhere there's a stacked run of big names at the Corn Exchange (Don Broco, Simon Amstell, Alison Moyet), Roachford and Hue and Cry co-headlining, and Cambridge's first UNIQLO and first cat café both open for business.

## Theme of the week: Remembering Syd Barrett

Syd Barrett, Pink Floyd's founding singer and guitarist, would have turned 80 this month — and Cambridge, his home town, marks the date with a concert, an exhibition, talks and even a football match.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · on sale since 26 Sep · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, at the venue where the Pink Floyd founder gave his last ever live performance in 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine headline a night of his songs, with lighting by The Mad Alchemist, in aid of mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · price not listed  
Original paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists exploring Barrett's art and cultural legacy. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett — A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's biographer Rob Chapman talks about his career and his book on the musician's life. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Meet the Director + Film Screening: Have You Got It Yet?** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cambridge (talk) & Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge (screening) · £10 (talk); cinema ticket price not listed  
Director Roddy Bogawa discusses making his 2023 documentary about Barrett and Pink Floyd, showing unseen outtakes, followed later that evening by a screening of the film itself. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United vs Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · price not listed  
An ordinary home league match with an unexpected tie to this week's theme: proceeds go to mental health charities, timed to mark Barrett's birthday, as noted by his sister in a recent interview. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
