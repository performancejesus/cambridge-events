# What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

*Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct*

Two weeks built around one big date: Cambridge marks 80 years since the birth of Syd Barrett with a concert, an exhibition and a biographer's talk, while the weekends bring Roachford and Hue and Cry, Nish Kumar and a first UNIQLO for the city.

## Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge marks the 80th anniversary of Syd Barrett's birth this year with a week of events built around the Pink Floyd founder — a tribute concert, an exhibition of his art, a biographer's talk and a screening of a documentary about him, with the director's introduction.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55 – £132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday this year, at the hall where the Pink Floyd founder gave his final live performance in 1972. Kula Shaker, Soft Machine and other acts perform his songs with a light show by The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — until 11 Oct · Openspace, Cambridge · prices on the website  
Original paintings and sketches by Barrett alongside work by contemporary artists responding to his art and cultural legacy. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Talk by Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett – A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, Cambridge · £10  
Barrett's biographer Rob Chapman, author of 'A Very Irregular Head', talks about his career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Meet the Director + Film Screening: Have You Got It Yet?** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace, Cambridge (talk); Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge (screening) · £10 (talk)  
Roddy Bogawa, director of the documentary 'Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd', talks about making the film and shows unseen outtakes at 5pm; the film itself screens at Arts Picturehouse at 8.30pm the same day. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
A League One fixture that, according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen, doubles as a mental health charity fundraiser this World Mental Health Day (10 October) — the club's own listing treats it as an ordinary home match. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
