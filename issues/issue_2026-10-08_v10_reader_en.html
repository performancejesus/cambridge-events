What's on in Cambridge — 8 Oct 2026

Events 8 Oct – 18 Oct · weekends: 10–11 Oct and 17–18 Oct

Two weeks built around one big date: Cambridge marks 80 years since the birth of Syd Barrett with a concert, an exhibition and a biographer's talk, while the weekends bring Roachford and Hue and Cry, Nish Kumar and a first UNIQLO for the city.

Theme of the week: Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge marks the 80th anniversary of Syd Barrett's birth this year with a week of events built around the Pink Floyd founder — a tribute concert, an exhibition of his art, a biographer's talk and a screening of a documentary about him, with the director's introduction.

A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations

Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55 – £132

A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday this year, at the hall where the Pink Floyd founder gave his final live performance in 1972. Kula Shaker, Soft Machine and other acts perform his songs with a light show by The Mad Alchemist; proceeds go to mental health charities.
