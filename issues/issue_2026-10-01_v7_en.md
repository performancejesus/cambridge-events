# What's on in Cambridge — 1 Oct 2026

*Events 1 Oct – 11 Oct · weekends: 3–4 Oct and 10–11 Oct*

Cambridge spends this fortnight marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday with a tribute concert, an exhibition and even a Cambridge United match for charity, while Roachford and Hue and Cry, Alison Moyet and Don Broco keep the Corn Exchange busy and a new Uniqlo and a cat café open their doors in town.

## Theme of the week: Remembering Syd Barrett at 80

Cambridge marks what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday this week with a tribute concert at the venue of his final gig, a small exhibition, a biographer's talk and a documentary screening — and, almost by accident, a football match too.

**A Tribute to Syd Barrett: Official 80th Anniversary Celebrations** — Sat 10 Oct · Cambridge Corn Exchange, Cambridge · £55–£132  
A concert marking what would have been Syd Barrett's 80th birthday, at the very venue where he gave his last live performance in 1972. Kula Shaker and Soft Machine headline a bill of artists reinterpreting his songs, with a light show by The Mad Alchemist. [More →](https://www.cornex.co.uk/events/a-tribute-to-syd-barrett-official-80th-anniversary-celebrations)

**Syd Barrett Exhibition: Life, Art and Cultural Impact** — 3–11 Oct · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · prices on the website  
Original paintings, sketches and work by contemporary artists exploring Barrett's art and cultural legacy, running for the week of the anniversary celebrations. [More →](https://www.cambridgeindependent.co.uk/whats-on/syd-barrett-s-sister-rosemary-breen-the-interest-that-s-st-9484462/)

**Rob Chapman: Syd Barrett, A Very Irregular Head** — Fri 9 Oct, 14:00 · Openspace, 54a Cherry Hinton Road, Cambridge · £10  
Acclaimed Barrett biographer Rob Chapman, author of 'A Very Irregular Head', talks about Barrett's life and career. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29037/)

**Meet the director + Have You Got It Yet? screening** — Fri 9 Oct, 17:00 and 20:30 · Openspace and Arts Picturehouse, Cambridge · £11.00  
Director Roddy Bogawa talks about making his documentary on Barrett and Pink Floyd and shows unseen outtakes at 5pm, followed the same evening by a screening of the finished film, 'Have You Got It Yet? The Story of Syd Barrett and Pink Floyd', at the Arts Picturehouse. [More →](https://wegottickets.com/f/29120)

**Cambridge United v Blackpool** — Sat 10 Oct, 15:00 · Abbey Stadium, Cambridge · prices on the website  
An ordinary home league fixture on paper, but according to Barrett's sister Rosemary Breen all proceeds from this match go to mental health charities — the date also happens to be World Mental Health Day. [More →](https://www.cambridgeunited.com/fixture/list/57)
